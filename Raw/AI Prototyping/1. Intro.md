# 


Week 1 introduces AI prototyping as a fundamental shift in product development, teaching you to build working software faster than traditional specs or wireframes. You'll create your first functional prototype, understand how this capability transforms the product lifecycle from sequential handoffs to collaborative iteration, and learn to navigate the landscape of AI coding tools to choose the right one for your needs. By the end of the week, you'll see prototypes not as end deliverables, but as essential decision-making tools for exploration, alignment, and validation.
